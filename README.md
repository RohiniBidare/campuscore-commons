## Executive Summary & Problem Understanding

Academic and institutional laboratories suffer from structural resource friction:
1. **Equipment Hoarding & Monopolization:** Well-funded research tracks frequently monopolize high-demand assets (e.g., Confocal Microscopes, Cleanroom Booths, GPU compute nodes) through bulk pre-booking, locking out undergraduate capstone and thesis students.
2. **Accountability Gaps & Unattributed Damage:** Equipment returns lack verifiable baseline comparisons. Damaged hardware leads to inter-departmental finger-pointing, slow dispute resolution, and unrepaired lab instruments.
3. **Audit Vulnerability:** Conventional database records can be manually altered or overwritten by local administrative accounts to expunge infractions or hide unauthorized usage.

**CampusCore Commons** solves this via a decentralized institutional governance architecture combining **algorithmic anti-monopoly scheduling**, **multimodal condition handshakes**, **LLM-assisted incident triage (Gemini API)**, and a **SHA-256 cryptographically chained audit ledger**.
